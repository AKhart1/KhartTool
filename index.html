Eight jobs, one drop

Filter a profile. The feedback role follows the card you pick.

Dataset

AI researchers & ML engineers

You are about to train. The folder is mixed sizes, leftover EXIF, and a few files that will not open.

Drop train/. This build resizes with Lanczos, strips metadata, and writes PNG, JPG, or WebP, up to 5,000 files. RGB normalization, corrupt-file discard, and a 512 center-crop are the v0.2 chain above — not a shipped dataloader.

RAW

Commercial & event photographers

Two thousand CR2 or ARW frames, and a client gallery that must not include the venue’s GPS pin.

Shift-drag a RAW file onto JPG, PNG, WEBP, or TIFF. Optional rawpy does the decode. Privacy Cleaner copies pixels into a clean file so GPS and camera serial stay behind. It is a wheel action, not a global hotkey.

Storefront

E-commerce & Shopify

A supplier dropped 15 MB TIFFs into the product folder. The store feels it on every page.

Web Optimizer resizes the long side to 1,920, strips EXIF, and writes WebP at quality 92. AVIF output and a fixed margin pass are not in 0.1.0 — ask for that preset below.

Confidential

Legal, healthcare & finance

A contract or record still carries an author name, a revision trail, or a GPS tag, and it cannot go to a conversion site.

The file stays on the PC. Strip image or PDF metadata, merge or compress PDFs, convert DOCX locally. This build does not flatten form fields, lock a PDF read-only, or certify HIPAA or GDPR. It also does not upload.

Social

Motion, YouTube & SMM

You need a looping GIF for a newsletter, or the audio from a clip, without a watermarked website.

Shift-drag to GIF, WebM, MP4, or Extract MP3. Ctrl opens trim, crop, speed, and mute through FFmpeg. A 15-second 9:16 60 fps GIF batch is the social preset above, not a one-flick export in this build.

Design

UI, UX & frontend

A Figma export folder is full of heavy PNGs and color profiles you do not want in the repo.

Drop the folder. Convert PNG to WebP, resize, strip profiles. KhartTool does not optimize SVG paths in 0.1.0 — that belongs on the feedback form, not in the download.

Docs

Writers, academics & educators

Lecture notes are in Markdown. A table is a CSV. You do not want Word, and you do not want an ad page.

Shift-drag between PDF, DOCX, TXT, JSON, CSV, and Markdown. DOCX to PDF uses Word when it is installed, otherwise a ReportLab approximation. JSON and CSV convert both ways.

Local

Privacy purists & power users

Another tab, another 25 MB cap, another site that keeps the file “for processing.”

The pill is already on the desktop. Shift converts, Ctrl opens tools, Esc closes the wheel. There is no global hotkey. The app sends nothing unless you start license activation yourself.
