Everyone has a file in the wrong shape.

You don't need to know what a codec is. If you can drag a file, you can use KhartTool. Pick who you are and see the chores it handles.

One drop. Every step.

A preset is a recipe: resize, convert, clean, rename, zip. Pick it once in the pill, then every file you drop goes through the whole chain. Your originals are never touched.

    Build your own in a minute

    Open the preset list, choose + New preset, add steps and drag them into order. The editor only offers steps that fit, so a chain can't break.

    • 37 steps to chainImages, audio, video, PDF and Word
    • Try on a fileSee the result before you save
    • Rename with a pattern{name}, {preset}, {n}, {date}
    • Share as .ktpresetA friend can import it in one click

    The free version keeps 2 custom presets plus every template that uses free steps.
